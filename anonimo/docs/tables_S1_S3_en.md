*Note.* The records of the type are those of the search whose title names the ecosystem type of the stream (for example, *entrepreneurial ecosystem*) and that have a reference list; their number appears in the final row of each stream. The most cited work was calculated over the 5,992 records that name some organizational ecosystem type in the title, counting the author in any position; for this reason, for some authors it is a work in which they appear as co-authors. The rank orders authors by the number of records of the type that cite them as first author; “—” indicates that the author does not appear in that position. The last column indicates how many core documents would stop meeting the rule if that author were removed from the list and, in the final row, if the whole stream were removed. Two cases deserve clarification. The rule searches for authors and not works, so an author active in several streams counts in the stream in which they appear even if the cited work belongs to another: the most cited works of Autio, a seed of innovation, are studies of entrepreneurial ecosystems, and removing Autio takes 40 documents (0.8%) out of the core. Van de Vrande does not appear in the references of the documents on knowledge ecosystems, and only one core document depends on this author; if Van de Vrande is replaced by Van der Borgh, whose study of knowledge-based ecosystems the reviews cite as a reference for that stream (Cobben et al., 2022), the core changes by three documents (two enter and one leaves). Source: own calculation on the Scopus export (semillas/rango\_semillas.csv, cobertura\_por\_semilla.csv, obras\_semillas.csv and quitar\_semillas.csv).

## Table S2. Alternative thresholds for the admission rule

| Admission rule | Records admitted | Records with an organizational ecosystem type in the title that are admitted | Records with ecological vocabulary in the title that are admitted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| At least one seed, from any stream | 6,535 | 71.9% | 143 |
| At least two seeds, from any stream | 5,327 | 64.1% | 25 |
| At least two seeds from the same stream (adopted rule) | 5,006 | 60.8% | 20 |
| At least three seeds from the same stream | 3,768 | 48.2% | 10 |

*Note.* The third column is the percentage of the 5,764 records with references that name an organizational ecosystem type in the title. Ecological vocabulary includes terms such as *biodiversity*, *wetland*, *coral* or *fisheries*, searched as whole words and excluding the terms that produce spurious matches in organizational titles (for example, *river* within *driver*). The 20 records admitted by the adopted rule are organizational studies on sectors such as fishing or forestry, or figurative uses; those added by the one-seed threshold include ecological studies proper, such as analyses of lakes or mountain vegetation. Source: own calculation (semillas/umbrales\_regla.csv).
